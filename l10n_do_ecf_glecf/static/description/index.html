e-CF DGII para Odoo

Send the invoices you post in Odoo to the DGII as electronic fiscal receipts (e-CF). Facturación electrónica e-CF para República Dominicana.

How it works

  1. From Settings, create a free GLECF account: the sign-up opens with your company name, RNC and address as configured in Odoo. Get your API key there.
  2. Paste the key in Settings > Invoicing > Electronic invoicing (e-CF).
  3. Upload your digital certificate (.p12) from the same screen. It goes straight to GLECF, encrypted; Odoo keeps neither the file nor its password.
  4. Accept sending your invoices to GLECF.
  5. Post your invoices as usual. Each e-CF is signed and sent to the DGII, and the answer comes back to the invoice.

What it does

  • Sends e-CF types 31, 32, 33, 34, 44 and 45 in Dominican pesos.
  • Checks the invoice before sending, so a missing RNC or expiry date is fixed in Odoo, not after a DGII rejection.
  • Fills the security code, signature date and QR code of the printed invoice, and keeps the signed XML.
  • Uses your company data in Odoo, and checks that the GLECF account has the same RNC before sending anything.
  • Uploads your digital certificate to GLECF from the Odoo settings.
  • Shows the DGII status on each invoice and in the invoice list.
  • Prevents resetting to draft an invoice already sent to the DGII.
  • Works on Odoo Community and Enterprise, on top of the Dominican localization (l10n_do_accounting).

External service and your data

This module needs a GLECF account. GLECF is the service that keeps your certificate, signs each e-CF and delivers it to the DGII. Nothing leaves Odoo until you accept it in Settings. When an e-CF is sent, its number, dates, amounts, lines and the data of your company and customer go to GLECF. Your invoices stay in your Odoo: if you stop using GLECF, nothing in Odoo is locked.

A free GLECF plan is available. Larger volumes need a paid GLECF plan, billed by GLECF, not through this module.

Requirements

  • Odoo 17.0, Community or Enterprise.
  • The Dominican fiscal localization l10n_do_accounting.
  • A GLECF account and a valid DGII digital certificate.
